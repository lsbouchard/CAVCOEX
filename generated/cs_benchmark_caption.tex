Equation-of-state predictions for a particle-sized hard-sphere cavity. The upper panel shows $\beta W_\mu(\sigma)=\beta\mu_{\rm ex}$; the lower panel shows $S(0)=[Z+\eta Z']^{-1}$ and $1-S(0)=\partial W_\mu/\partial\mu$. Curves are analytic EOS predictions, not independent simulation measurements. The vertical line and uncertainty band give $\eta_f=0.491882(46)$, derived from the extrapolated coexisting-fluid specific volume of Fernandez et al. The mKLM fluid EOS is continued above coexistence only to $\eta=0.52$, within its documented fit range ($\rho\sigma^3\leq1.02$, or $\eta\leq0.5341$); that segment is a metastable-fluid continuation.
